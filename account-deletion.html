Account Deletion

Last updated: July 23, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of ClassSeats account, license, and operational records associated with your educator email address.

How to Request Deletion

To request deletion, send an email to support@classseats.com from the email address associated with your ClassSeats account. Include the subject line "ClassSeats account deletion request."

If you cannot send from the account email address, include enough information for us to verify that you are authorized to make the request.

What We Delete

After verifying the request, ClassWare Solutions, LLC will delete or de-identify ClassSeats-controlled records associated with the account, such as:

  • ClassSeats account or sign-in records tied to the email address
  • License, trial, or entitlement records tied to the account
  • Operational telemetry records that are reasonably associated with the account

Teacher-Owned Classroom Files

ClassSeats classroom files, student photos, student audio, notes, attendance records, and seating charts are stored on your device and/or in your selected Google Drive account. These files are controlled by you, not by ClassWare Solutions, LLC.

Deleting your ClassSeats account records does not automatically delete classroom files from Google Drive or from your own devices. You can delete those files directly using Google Drive or standard device tools.

Timing and Retention

We will respond to account deletion requests within a reasonable time. Some records may be retained if needed for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or other legitimate business purposes.

Questions

For questions about account deletion or data handling, contact support@classseats.com.
